# Business brief — Wrenfield Nutrition

**Business:** a fictional online nutrition coaching practice for U.S. adults taking a GLP-1 weight-loss medication (semaglutide, tirzepatide), or coming off one. Coaching runs by video across the U.S. It works alongside the client's prescriber and never gives medication or dosing advice.

**Why this niche of nutrition coaching:**
- **High demand:** a 2026 Gallup National Health and Well-Being Index poll (5,065 adults) found 11% of U.S. adults taking a GLP-1 for weight loss, up from 3% two years earlier, and about 15% have used one at some point. A small cross-sectional study of GLP-1 users (n=69, Frontiers in Nutrition) found low protein, fiber and micronutrient intake, and only 20% had been referred to a dietitian. Trial participants usually had dietitian and exercise support that isn't always available in the community.
- **Too much supply:** nutrition and weight-management coaching is the largest segment of the health-coach market (about 34%, Towards Healthcare). Online nutrition coaching is rated "high competitive pressure" by market screeners, and coaching is lightly regulated, so many low-end, generic "macro coach" offers compete. No source counted U.S. nutrition coaches, so "too much supply" rests on market share and low barriers to entry, not a headcount. GLP-1 nutrition coaching itself is a small, fast-growing segment (Fact.MR: about $125M in 2026, 18.5% a year).
- **Why the website matters:** buyers can't tell a careful, medication-aware coach from a generic diet seller. Clear scope ("we work alongside your prescriber"), plain pricing and a useful self-check are how a practice stands out.

**Business model the site supports:** a free 20-minute fit call leads to a 12-week "Steady Strength" program, a shorter 4-week start, or a "Taper & Maintain" plan for people coming off the medication.

**Funnel (3+ steps):** "Plate Check" self-assessment (7 questions) → result, weakest areas and recommended plan → 4-step fit-call request (where you are → goals → contact and time → review) → success. A free "GLP-1 Protein Plate Guide" email capture catches people not ready to book.

**Brand direction:** fresh, calm and kitchen-real. Cream, deep sage green, warm terracotta for actions, a soft wheat panel color; a small wren mark.

**Claims to avoid:** medication or dosing advice, guaranteed weight loss, before/after numbers, invented credentials, testimonials or statistics, and anything that presents coaching as medical care.
